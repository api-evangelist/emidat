> Emidat GmbH is a Munich-based climate technology company building product intelligence infrastructure for the construction industry. Its SaaS platform lets construction material manufacturers create verified Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs), run Life Cycle Assessments (LCAs), and produce Digital Product Passports (DPPs) at scale, generating ISO-compliant verified EPDs in as little as a day. Emidat has no public API or developer portal at this time; integrations are delivered as part of the enterprise platform.
